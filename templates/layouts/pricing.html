How pricing works

Fixed price, agreed up front

The price is agreed before any work starts, and it is final. If development takes longer than expected, you are not charged extra.

Deposit and contract

A deposit is required before work begins, usually a third of the total. We sign a short service contract, then I invoice.

Progress you can see

You get regular updates on what has been completed, with the chance to suggest changes early — before they get expensive.

Handover and source code

After the final invoice you receive the source code and full documentation. Keep a local backup; do not rely on mine.
